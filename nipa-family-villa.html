Nipa Family
Pool Villa

Inspired by Crafted With Nature, the Nipa Family Pool Villa offers a private setting for families to gather among the gentle movement of the nipa palms.

Two bedrooms are connected by shared living spaces that open towards the private pool, terrace and outdoor dining area. With room to come together and quieter corners to retreat to, the villa allows breakfasts to linger, children to move freely and time to unfold naturally throughout the day.

Available with a king or twin-bed configuration, each villa includes:

  • • Indoor living space: 80 sqm / 861 sq ft
  • • Private pool and terrace: 130 sqm / 1,399 sq ft
  • • Total villa space: 210 sqm / 2,260 sq ft
