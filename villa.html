Honeymoon
Pool Villa

Inspired by Rituals Of Light, the Honeymoon Pool Villa is shaped around privacy, togetherness and the changing glow of the river throughout the day.

The bedroom opens towards the private pool and river beyond, while a private Jacuzzi and sauna invite slower moments shared between two. From quiet mornings to evenings held in soft light, the villa creates an intimate setting with space to be entirely together.

Available with a king or twin-bed configuration, each villa includes:

  • • Indoor living space: 62 sqm / 667 sq ft
  • • Private pool and terrace: 86 sqm / 926 sq ft
  • • Total villa space: 148 sqm / 1,593 sq ft
